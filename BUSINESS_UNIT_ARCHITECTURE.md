# Distribution System - Business Unit Architecture Guide\n\n## Overview\n\nThe Distribution System is designed with a dual-business unit architecture to handle different operational requirements:\n\n- **VISMASS**: VAT-compliant operations (Import/Buying/Selling, Services, Main Stock)\n- **MALIBO**: Non-VAT operations (Delivery, Printing)\n\n## Business Unit Structure\n\n### VISMASS (VAT Operations)\n\n**Purpose**: Handles complex VAT-compliant business operations\n\n**Core Functions**:\n- Import and Buying operations\n- Sales with VAT calculations\n- Service job management\n- Main stock inventory management\n- VAT reporting and compliance\n\n**Key Features**:\n- Full VAT integration with configurable rates\n- Complex batch management system\n- Multi-level pricing (Cost, Retail, Wholesale, etc.)\n- Service job tracking with customer management\n- Comprehensive stock transfers between main stock locations\n- VAT-compliant reporting\n\n**Access Control**:\n- Users with company codes starting with 'VIS' (e.g., VIS001)\n- Users assigned to main stock sections (`is_main_stock = true`)\n- Users with VAT-related permissions\n\n### MALIBO (Non-VAT Operations)\n\n**Purpose**: Handles delivery and printing operations without VAT complexity\n\n**Core Functions**:\n- Delivery management and tracking\n- Route optimization\n- Printing operations and stock management\n- Barcode generation\n- Distribution performance tracking\n\n**Key Features**:\n- Advanced delivery route optimization\n- Real-time delivery tracking\n- Printing-specific inventory management\n- Barcode generation and printing\n- Non-VAT customer management\n- Performance analytics for distribution\n\n**Access Control**:\n- Users with company codes starting with 'MAL' (e.g., MAL001)\n- Users assigned to non-main stock sections (`is_main_stock = false`)\n- Users with delivery/printing permissions\n\n## Technical Architecture\n\n### Middleware Protection\n\nThe `BusinessUnitMiddleware` enforces access control:\n\n```php\n// Example route protection\nRoute::middleware(['auth', 'business.unit:vismass'])->group(function () {\n    // VISMASS routes\n});\n\nRoute::middleware(['auth', 'business.unit:malibo'])->group(function () {\n    // MALIBO routes  \n});\n```\n\n### Route Structure\n\n**VISMASS Routes** (`/vismass/*`):\n- `/vismass/purchases/*` - Import/Buying operations\n- `/vismass/sales/*` - Sales with VAT\n- `/vismass/services/*` - Service jobs\n- `/vismass/stock/*` - Main stock management\n- `/vismass/vat/*` - VAT rate management\n- `/vismass/reports/*` - VAT-compliant reports\n\n**MALIBO Routes** (`/malibo/*`):\n- `/malibo/deliveries/*` - Delivery management\n- `/malibo/routes/*` - Route optimization\n- `/malibo/printing/*` - Printing operations\n- `/malibo/distribution/*` - Distribution management\n- `/malibo/customers/*` - Non-VAT customers\n- `/malibo/reports/*` - Distribution reports\n\n### Database Design\n\n**Shared Tables**:\n- `users` - User management\n- `companies` - Company information\n- `sections` - Organizational units\n- `itemmaster` - Product catalog (with business unit separation)\n\n**VISMASS-Specific Tables**:\n- `vat_rates` - VAT configuration\n- `sales_transactions` - VAT sales\n- `service_jobs` - Service management\n\n**MALIBO-Specific Tables**:\n- `deliveries` - Delivery tracking\n- `delivery_routes` - Route management\n- `barcode_print_batches` - Printing jobs\n\n## User Guide\n\n### Getting Started\n\n1. **User Assignment**: Users are assigned to specific business units based on:\n   - Company code (VIS* for VISMASS, MAL* for MALIBO)\n   - Section assignment (main stock vs. printing/delivery)\n   - Permission-based access\n\n2. **Navigation**: The system automatically shows relevant menus based on business unit access\n\n3. **Dual Access**: Some users may have access to both business units if they have appropriate permissions\n\n### VISMASS Operations\n\n#### Purchase Management\n1. Navigate to `/vismass/purchases`\n2. Create purchase orders with:\n   - Supplier selection\n   - Batch number assignment\n   - VAT calculations\n   - Main stock allocation\n\n#### Sales Operations\n1. Access `/vismass/sales`\n2. Process sales with:\n   - Customer VAT details\n   - Batch selection\n   - Automatic VAT calculation\n   - Receipt generation\n\n#### Service Jobs\n1. Go to `/vismass/services`\n2. Create service jobs with:\n   - Customer device details\n   - Service charges\n   - Payment tracking\n   - Job status updates\n\n#### Stock Management\n1. Visit `/vismass/stock`\n2. Manage inventory with:\n   - Batch tracking\n   - Stock transfers\n   - Low stock alerts\n   - Stock valuation\n\n### MALIBO Operations\n\n#### Delivery Management\n1. Navigate to `/malibo/deliveries`\n2. Create and track deliveries:\n   - Customer details (no VAT requirements)\n   - Route assignment\n   - Status tracking\n   - Delivery confirmation\n\n#### Route Optimization\n1. Access `/malibo/routes`\n2. Optimize delivery routes:\n   - Add stops and destinations\n   - Calculate optimal paths\n   - Assign drivers\n   - Monitor performance\n\n#### Printing Operations\n1. Go to `/malibo/printing`\n2. Manage printing tasks:\n   - Stock management for printing supplies\n   - Barcode generation\n   - Job scheduling\n   - Quality control\n\n### Batch Management\n\n#### Understanding Batches\nBatches allow tracking of product groups with:\n- Unique batch numbers\n- Individual pricing per batch\n- Expiry date tracking\n- Quantity management\n\n#### Creating Batches\n1. Access batch management through product pages\n2. Click \"New Batch\" button\n3. Fill batch details:\n   - Batch number (required)\n   - Quantity\n   - Cost and selling prices\n   - Expiry date (optional)\n\n#### Managing Batches\n- Search batches by number or product name\n- Filter by status (active, expired, low stock)\n- View batch history and transactions\n- Update batch information\n\n## API Documentation\n\n### Authentication\nAll API endpoints require authentication:\n```http\nAuthorization: Bearer {token}\n```\n\n### VISMASS Endpoints\n\n#### Create Purchase\n```http\nPOST /vismass/purchases\nContent-Type: application/json\n\n{\n  \"supplier_code\": \"SUP001\",\n  \"items\": [\n    {\n      \"item_code\": \"ITEM001\",\n      \"batch_no\": \"BATCH001\",\n      \"quantity\": 10,\n      \"cost_price\": 50.00\n    }\n  ]\n}\n```\n\n#### Create Sale with VAT\n```http\nPOST /vismass/sales\nContent-Type: application/json\n\n{\n  \"customer_name\": \"ABC Company\",\n  \"items\": [\n    {\n      \"product_id\": 1,\n      \"quantity\": 2,\n      \"unit_price\": 100.00,\n      \"vat_inclusive\": false\n    }\n  ],\n  \"vat_rate\": 15.00\n}\n```\n\n### MALIBO Endpoints\n\n#### Create Delivery\n```http\nPOST /malibo/deliveries\nContent-Type: application/json\n\n{\n  \"customer_name\": \"Quick Print\",\n  \"customer_address\": \"123 Main St\",\n  \"items\": [\n    {\n      \"item_name\": \"Business Cards\",\n      \"quantity\": 1000,\n      \"unit_price\": 0.50\n    }\n  ]\n}\n```\n\n#### Optimize Route\n```http\nPOST /malibo/routes/{id}/optimize\nContent-Type: application/json\n\n{\n  \"optimization_criteria\": \"distance\",\n  \"max_stops_per_trip\": 5\n}\n```\n\n## Troubleshooting\n\n### Access Denied Errors\nIf you receive \"Access denied to [BUSINESS_UNIT] operations\":\n1. Check your company code assignment\n2. Verify section assignments\n3. Contact administrator for permission updates\n\n### Route Not Found\nIf routes are not accessible:\n1. Clear application cache: `php artisan cache:clear`\n2. Ensure middleware is properly registered\n3. Check route registration in `bootstrap/app.php`\n\n### Batch Management Issues\n1. Ensure product exists before creating batches\n2. Verify unique batch numbers per product\n3. Check section permissions for batch operations\n\n## Development Notes\n\n### Adding New Business Unit Features\n\n1. **Create Controller**: Add to appropriate business unit namespace\n2. **Add Routes**: Update `routes/vismass.php` or `routes/malibo.php`\n3. **Middleware**: Ensure proper business unit protection\n4. **Tests**: Add tests to corresponding test files\n5. **Frontend**: Create components in business unit folders\n\n### Database Migrations\n\nWhen adding business unit-specific features:\n```php\n// Add company_code and section_code for separation\nSchema::table('new_table', function (Blueprint $table) {\n    $table->string('company_code')->index();\n    $table->string('section_code')->nullable()->index();\n    $table->boolean('is_vat_applicable')->default(false);\n});\n```\n\n### Frontend Components\n\nOrganize components by business unit:\n```\nresources/js/components/\n├── vismass/\n│   ├── Sales/\n│   ├── Purchases/\n│   └── Services/\n├── malibo/\n│   ├── Deliveries/\n│   ├── Printing/\n│   └── Routes/\n└── shared/\n    ├── batch/\n    └── common/\n```\n\n## Security Considerations\n\n1. **Middleware Protection**: All business unit routes are protected\n2. **Permission-Based Access**: Users need appropriate permissions\n3. **Data Segregation**: Business units cannot access each other's sensitive data\n4. **Audit Trail**: All operations are logged with business unit context\n\n## Performance Optimization\n\n1. **Database Indexing**: Company and section codes are indexed\n2. **Caching**: Business unit permissions are cached\n3. **Query Optimization**: Filters are applied at database level\n4. **Route Optimization**: Separate route files reduce loading time\n\n## Maintenance\n\n### Regular Tasks\n1. Monitor business unit performance metrics\n2. Update VAT rates as needed (VISMASS)\n3. Optimize delivery routes regularly (MALIBO)\n4. Clean up expired batches\n5. Review user access permissions\n\n### Backup Considerations\n- Separate backup strategies for each business unit if needed\n- Ensure batch data integrity\n- Maintain VAT compliance records\n- Preserve delivery tracking history
